# Privacy Policy for Cadence — Habit & Performance Tracker

**Last Updated:** October 25, 2026

Cadence ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and protection when you use the Cadence Chrome Extension.

---

### 1. Single Purpose & Data Minimization
Cadence has a single purpose: to help users build consistent daily habits, maintain focused work sprints, and track personal productivity performance from every new browser tab. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver this service.

---

### 2. Information We Collect
* **Account Information:** If you choose to create an account or sign in with Google OAuth, we collect your email address and a unique user ID strictly for authentication, account management, and verifying Cadence Pro subscription entitlements.
* **Habits & Preferences:** Your habit definitions, completion streaks, timer settings, custom wallpapers, and soundscape preferences are saved locally on your device (`chrome.storage.local`) and optionally synced securely to your private Supabase database account.
* **Payment Information:** All subscription transactions are processed directly by certified third-party payment processors (Stripe or Razorpay). Cadence servers and the extension never collect, handle, or store credit card numbers, bank details, or UPI credentials.

---

### 3. Information We DO NOT Collect
* **Browsing History:** We do NOT track, record, or store the websites you visit or your browsing activity.
* **Website Content:** The Focus Guard site blocker works strictly locally inside your browser to block distracting tabs during active Pomodoro sessions; no webpage contents or URLs are transmitted to any server.
* **Personal Communications or Keystrokes:** We never record mouse movements, keystrokes, personal chats, or form data.

---

### 4. Data Sharing & Third Parties
* **We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties under any circumstances.**
* **We do not use your data for advertising, marketing profiling, or creditworthiness evaluation.**
* Data is only communicated with the following necessary service endpoints:
  * Google OAuth / Supabase: For secure user authentication and habit backup.
  * Unsplash API (`images.unsplash.com`): For retrieving high-resolution wallpaper imagery.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion
You can delete your local habits, streaks, and preferences at any time by clearing extension data or uninstalling the extension. To request deletion of your account and synced cloud data, contact us at `maheshpawar75075@gmail.com`.
